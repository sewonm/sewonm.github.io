Mathematical Research

geometric generative modeling on manifolds

Ongoing

Investigating generative models defined on Riemannian manifolds and discrete geometric structures. Current focus includes heat kernel methods on symmetric spaces, conformal parameterizations of triangulated surfaces, and Laplacian-based kernels on cellular complexes.

Reading Lou et al. (Riemannian diffusion models on symmetric spaces)
Reading Dorobantu et al. (conformal generative modeling)
Reading Alain et al. (Gaussian processes on cellular complexes)

Directed Reading — Knot Sliceness & Low-Dimensional Topology

Semester-long directed study on knot sliceness and 4-manifold topology. Worked through geometric and algebraic techniques underlying the proof that the Conway knot is not slice and presented findings in a formal talk.

Computational Geometry

Discrete Differential Geometry Playground

(Polyscope / Python / C++ project)

Implementing and visualizing geometric operators on triangulated meshes, including Laplace–Beltrami operators, heat diffusion, and spectral decompositions.

Single-Image 3D Reconstruction Study

Exploring geometry-aware approaches to reconstructing 3D structure from 2D projections, including implicit neural representations and mesh-based methods.

Creative Technology

easel: AI Art teacher

An AI-assisted art education system designed to provide structured, technique-focused feedback on drawings and paintings. The project explores how large language models and vision models can function as interactive studio mentors, offering critique grounded in composition, perspective, lighting, and form. Easel emphasizes interpretable feedback rather than purely generative output, framing AI as a pedagogical tool rather than a replacement for artistic practice.
